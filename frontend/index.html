A simple way to agree on work

Start with a verified phone number. Agree on the work and rate together before the job begins.

1
Create your free work record

Verify your phone and add a few details about the work you do. No CV is needed. Your Work Passport can start with your first recorded job.

Verified phone number
2
Meet and confirm the start code.

Meet in person and enter the four-digit start code together. This marks when the agreed work begins.

Confirm the job start together
3
Finish, get paid cash, and record the work.

When the work is done, the customer pays you directly in cash. Enter the finish code together and add a rating to the Work Passport.

Cash paid on completion
